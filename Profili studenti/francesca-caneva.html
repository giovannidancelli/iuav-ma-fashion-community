Tessence

Francesca Caneva, Heritage · Memory · Emotions, Living Archive

AUTHOR ↺

Tessence is a sensory exhibition that brings together art, matter and critical thinking to reflect on the crisis in the textile sector and the possibilities of regeneration through ecodesign. The project transforms pre and post-consumer waste into multisensory installations, addressing the environmental impact of fashion across emissions, water consumption and microplastics, and proposing a cultural and creative response to waste culture. Its main objective is to redefine the value of materials, promote circularity and rebuild a conscious connection between production, consumption and the regeneration of matter. The experience invites the public to enter a narrative ecosystem where every fibre becomes memory, every offcut a possibility and every artisanal gesture an act of responsibility towards a collective regenerative future.
